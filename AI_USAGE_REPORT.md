TRADE-OFFS
JWT in Frontend Storage

JWT authentication was implemented for the assignment prototype.

For a production system, HttpOnly cookies and refresh-token handling could provide stronger protection against certain client-side token exposure risks.

Simple Role-Based Authorization

The prototype implements role-based authentication and dashboard behavior.

A production system could introduce more fine-grained authorization policies at the individual endpoint and resource level.

Neon PostgreSQL

Neon PostgreSQL was used instead of a local PostgreSQL installation to provide a cloud-hosted PostgreSQL database that could be accessed consistently during development.

Simple SLA Calculation

SLA due times are calculated from ticket priority.

A production system could additionally account for business hours, holidays, escalation rules, and notification workflows.
